# JPC Portal — Employee User Guide

How the **Employee** role uses the system, from login to report approval.

> Demo account: `employee@jpc.local` / `P@ssw0rd2026` (dev only).

## 1. Login

Open the portal and sign in with your credentials. Your **employee dashboard**
loads with your tasks, targets and recent activity.

## 2. View your dashboard

The employee dashboard shows:

- assigned tasks and their progress
- your targets and progress toward them
- recent reports and their statuses
- notifications

## 3. View assigned tasks

- Open **My Tasks** (or the dashboard list).
- Each task shows title, priority, due date, status and progress percentage.
- Open a task to see references, comments, attachments, activity timeline and the
  progress history.

## 4. View targets

- Open **Targets** to see your assigned targets for the academic year: title,
  value, unit and completion status.

## 5. Create a report

1. Open the report area → **New report**.
2. Choose the related task (if applicable), enter title and content.
3. The report starts in `draft` status — you can keep editing while it is a draft.

## 6. Upload a document

1. In the report, attach your document (PDF/DOCX/images within the size limit).
2. The file is stored **privably** — only authorized users can download it.
3. On upload the OCR job (`ProcessReportOcr*`) extracts the document text.

## 7. Review AI analysis

After upload/processing you can view the AI-generated **summary** and **findings**
(overview, validation checks, reference/similarity indicators).

- These are **assistive indicators only** — they do not decide your report's
  outcome.
- If you disagree with a finding, note it when you submit or discuss it with your
  reviewer.

## 8. Submit the report

1. Review your content and attachments.
2. Click **Submit** → status changes `draft` → `submitted` (workflow-validated; a
   submitted version is immutable and a new version is created if you resubmit).
3. You receive a notification when the reviewer acts.

## 9. Track review

- The report detail page shows the **timeline** (submitted, processing, under
  review, …) and any reviewer comments.
- Statuses you will see: `submitted`, `processing`, `under_review`,
  `revision_required`, `resubmitted`, `approved`, `rejected`.

## 10. Respond to a revision

If the reviewer sets **revision required**:

1. Open the report → read reviewer comments.
2. Edit content / replace files (a new version is recorded).
3. **Resubmit** → status becomes `resubmitted` and it re-enters review.

## 11. Resubmit

Repeat step 10 as needed; each submission creates a new immutable version with its
own timeline entry. You can compare versions from the report detail view.

## 12. View your performance

- **Performance** — your performance records, score breakdowns, completion rate and
  progress snapshots for the period/academic year.

## 13. View your achievements

- **Achievements** — badges earned, monthly achievements and rank (employee of the
  month when applicable).

## 14. View notifications

- **Notifications** — in-app notifications for assignments, deadline reminders,
  overdue alerts, review decisions and achievements; set channel preferences.
